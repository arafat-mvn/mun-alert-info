Data System Bottlenecks & Operational Gaps

Critical reporting vulnerabilities identified during dataset review that impede child recovery

SLA Bottleneck High Priority

Unapproved Status Stagnation

A high percentage of urgent reports remain marked `approved: false` indefinitely, delaying broadcast during the vital first 2 hours.

Target Fix: Implement 2-Hour SLA for emergency verification.
Duplicate Logs 2–4x Multi-Entry

Lack of Case De-Duplication

Multiple family members log the same child with slight spelling variances ("Rafiqun Nabi" vs "Rohomot"), fragmenting police dispatch tracking.

Target Fix: Unified National Case ID auto-merging.
GIS Blindspot No Coordinates

Free-Text Location Logging

Absence of standardized lat/long GPS points prevents automated geo-fenced alert triggers and live GIS spatial heat mapping.

Target Fix: Integrated location picker on intake form.
Resolution Gap Stuck Active

Missing Recovery Metrics

Cases remain permanently marked as "active" with no systematic follow-up to record whether a child was found, obscuring true recovery rates.

Target Fix: Automated 48-hour case resolution check-in.
Chain of Custody Unverified

Unassigned Police Contacts

Case entries list generic mobile numbers without linking to an official police officer of record or designated local thana dispatch desk.

Target Fix: Thana-wise assigned officer field.
Response Lag Untracked

Uncomputed Reporting Delay

The system fails to calculate the time gap between `date` (disappearance) and `reportedAt`, preventing automated priority queueing for fresh cases.

Target Fix: Auto-calculated Response Lag Index.

Stakeholder Action Playbooks

Tailored operational protocols for parents, police, and civil authorities

Primary Risk Factors

  • Solo travel to/from madrasa, tuition, or market during dusk (4–8 PM) or early morning (5–7 AM).
  • Unsupervised play near high-hazard zones: canals, riverfronts, bridges, or railway tracks.
  • Diffusion of adult supervision during crowded events (weddings, village melas, markets).
  • Open courtyard exposure for toddlers in high-density or market-adjacent homes.

Proactive Safeguards

  • 30-Minute Check Rule: Treat a 30-minute delay from madrasa/school as an immediate alert trigger.
  • Buddy Commuting: Enforce mandatory group travel along transit-hub or bridge routes.
  • Bi-Annual Photo Vault: Keep an updated clear photograph on phone; many alert entries lacked pictures.
  • Wearable Identification: Attach stitched name/contact tags to toddler clothing in market areas.

First 0–60 Minutes Protocol

  1. Immediate Filing: Do NOT wait 24 hours. Contact police and file MUN alert immediately.
  2. Route Retrace: Retrace route (Home → Madrasa/Market/Bridge) and check with classmates/shopkeepers.
  3. Mosque Loudspeaker: Utilize local mosque PA systems for instant neighborhood broadcast.
  4. Check High-Risk Hazards: Immediately check nearby transit hubs, rail lines, and canal/water edges.

Deployment Strategy

  • Increase foot & bike patrols at madrasa gates during peak release hours (4–8 PM & 5–7 AM).
  • Station dedicated rapid-response officers at major transit hubs (Kamalapur, Sadarghat, Chashara).
  • Prioritize community policing outreach in industrial belts (Gazipur, Savar, Narayanganj).

Investigative Priorities

  • Madrasa Cases: Canvas institution staff, classmates, and transport/rickshaw drivers along routine paths immediately.
  • Teen Disappearances (13–17 yrs): Run dual-track inquiries covering abduction and voluntary departure (runaway/marriage).
  • Toddler Cases: Treat water hazards (canals/wells) within 300m radius as immediate search priorities.

Inter-Agency Coordination

  • Establish automated alert pipelines to bus, launch, and rail station master offices to intercept traveling minors.
  • Implement a single case-ID system to auto-merge duplicate filings from multiple family members.
  • Maintain active liaison desks with Rohingya camp management (RRRC/NGOs) in Cox's Bazar.

Infrastructure & Safety

  • Install high-intensity lighting and CCTV coverage at madrasa gates, market entrances, and bridges.
  • Erect protective fencing along open canals and steep riverfront steps near residential colonies.
  • Establish designated "Lost Child Help Desks" at major bus/launch/rail terminals.

Emergency Alert Optimization

  • Verification SLA: Enforce a strict 2-hour maximum SLA for alert verification (`approved: true`).
  • Geo-Fenced Alerts: Deploy targeted SMS broadcasts to mobile phones within a 3km radius of last seen locations.
  • Unified Hotline: Consolidate alert intake into a single verified national number with case de-duplication logic.

Social Support Programs

  • Fund school and madrasa safe-commute initiatives (staggered dismissals, parent volunteers).
  • Expand youth counseling and runaway-prevention mediation services in industrial zones.
  • Standardize child registration and ID programs in high-vulnerability Rohingya refugee camps.
